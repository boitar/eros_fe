# Eros-FE v1.11.2

This release fixes the blank mobile/narrow-window sign-in screen and adds gallery version lineage, incremental download updates, and iOS background download keep-alive.

- **Fix blank sign-in screen on Android, iOS, and narrow desktop windows:** Expand the login scaffold and curtain stack to the full viewport and clamp the bottom form sheet height on short screens so sign-in fields and actions always remain visible (Fixes [#9](https://github.com/boitar/eros_fe/issues/9), PR [#10](https://github.com/boitar/eros_fe/pull/10)).
- **iOS background gallery downloads:** Keep downloads alive in the background with iOS 26+ native `BGContinuedProcessingTask` live progress, non-interrupting audio keep-alive fallback, `beginBackgroundTask` safety net, and silent local completion notifications, with new iOS settings toggles (PR [#8](https://github.com/boitar/eros_fe/pull/8)).
- **Gallery version lineage & incremental download updates:** Parse and navigate `Parent` and `Newer Versions` links from gallery detail and info pages, check downloaded galleries for newer versions, and upgrade incrementally while reusing existing images and preserving reading progress.

本次发布修复移动端与窄窗登录页空白问题，并新增画廊版本血缘、已下载画廊增量更新及 iOS 后台下载保活：

- **修复登录页空白（Android / iOS / 窄窗桌面）：** 补齐 `LoginPage` 全屏展开约束、顶栏定位层级与极矮视口高度保护，避免下半屏登录表单坍缩至 `0px` 仅剩右上角出口地区胶囊（关联并修复 [#9](https://github.com/boitar/eros_fe/issues/9)，PR [#10](https://github.com/boitar/eros_fe/pull/10)）。
- **iOS 后台画廊下载保活与完成通知：** 支持 iOS 26+ 原生 `BGContinuedProcessingTask` 灵动岛/锁屏实时进度、不打断音乐/播客的静音混音保活回退与后台完成无声本地通知，并在下载设置新增 iOS 专属开关（PR [#8](https://github.com/boitar/eros_fe/pull/8)）。
- **画廊版本血缘与增量更新：** 支持解析并跳转父画廊（Parent）与更新版本链（Newer Versions），在下载页检测已下载画廊的新版本并复用本地已有图片原子增量升级，保留阅读进度。

Android 提供对应 ABI 的分架构安装包与通用包；请按设备架构选择。详细说明见 [README_cn.md](../README_cn.md)。
